🍽️ Restaurants — All Walk-In

No dinner reservations needed on this trip — every meal is a flexible window with a few options to choose from on the day
New approach: This is a sightseeing-first trip, so nothing hinges on a restaurant booking. Each meal in the itinerary is now a time window with 3–4 walk-in options (tap the ⓘ on any meal in the itinerary to see the list). Show up, pick whichever has a table, and if one spot is full the next good place is usually a block away. A couple of the popular spots take reservations if you want the certainty, but none are required. Just verify opening days/hours on the day — some close one day a week or for August holidays (many Italian spots close part of August for ferie).
